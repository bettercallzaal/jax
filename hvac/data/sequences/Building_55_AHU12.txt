Air Handler Interlocks:
Software interlocks will not allow the exhaust Fans to run unless status is made on the supply fans. 
Air Handler Safeties: 
Unit is equipped with manual reset high static pressure limits.
High Static Pressure will shut down, and prevent units from starting, until the manual reset is pressed.  
Smoke detection shut down safety circuit.
Will shut down, and prevent the unit from starting. (Will not evacuate the building.) 


Freeze Protection
Freeze protection will lock the unit out on low temperature, and will not allow the unit to run. 
Fan Fail Reset
When the unit fails to start after a length of time, the fan fail reset will need to be reset on Metasys, before the unit will start.
Sequence of Operations (Start/Stop/Pressure):
The air handler will be commanded “ON” by the commands located on Extended Architecture.
Outside air dampers will modulate until end switches are made. 
Supply fans are turned on and will begin to increase in speed. 
Once fan status is made isolation dampers and exhaust fans will be commanded on. 
Supply fans will modulate speed based on Discharge Static Pressure Control.
 Once status is made on the exhaust fans, isolation dampers will open. 
Temperature Control:
Temperature is controlled by discharge air temperature set point located on Extended Architecture.
Cooling will be locked out if the Outdoor Air Temperature is below the cooling enable set point. 
Heat/Cooling Reclaim will be available when temperatures are below 50 and above 75 based on Outdoor Air Temperature. 
Reclaim will modulate to maintain a discharge air temperature to pre-heat/cool the incoming outdoor air to set point.  
The preheat coil will modulate 1/3, 2/3 valves as needed to maintain discharge air temperature set point.
Face and Bypass will switch based on heat reclaim leaving are temperature as compared to Face and Bypass set point. 
When on face and bypass control, the 1/3, 2/3 valves will be commanded to 100%, and temperature will be modulated using the bypass dampers. 
Hot/Chilled Water/Control Air/Controls:
The mouse room reheats are served from Bldg. 1 Unit 1 Room 1275. (Old Generator Room.) controlled by “1055005CGE Heat Exchanger” under the SNE01 controller on the BACnet IP.
Chilled water is fed from pumps 3 & 4 located in Building 50 room B-101-A controlled by “16A023FEC Chilled Water System 1” and “16A022FEC Chilled Water System 2” under the SNE01 controller on the BACnet IP. Chillers Start at 52° outside air temperature.
Control Air is fed from Building 22 off of the Rotary, with a redundant air compressor located in Building 55 room 1370. 
The units are controlled by the SNE-01 Network Device, on the BACnet IP with AHU-1 on “1055000CGE AHU-1” Controller and AHU-2 on “1055001CGE AHU-2” Controller.
